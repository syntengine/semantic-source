## 📜 VERIFIED SPECIFICATION (SUMMARY OF STEPS)

* **STEP 1: Universal Human Insight** — Define the underlying human need independent of product or category.
* **STEP 2: Cultural Dynamics** — Identify how culture amplifies, guides, or reshapes that need.
* **STEP 3: PEST Filtering** — Use Political, Economic, Social, and Technological conditions to understand where the need becomes a viable opportunity space.
* **STEP 4: Emergent Demand Clusters** — Look beyond demographics; identify by need intensity and readiness.
* **STEP 5: Opportunity Definition & Prioritization** — Criteria: Need intensity, Adoption readiness, Value perception.
* **STEP 6: Value Architecture** — Functional value, Emotional value (increases autonomy), Cultural value.
* **STEP 7: Strategic Positioning** — Cultural assumptions, evolving behaviours, systemic gaps, emerging cultural currents.
* **STEP 8: Ecosystem Integration** — Define how the solution plugs into a broader value ecosystem (who it complements, elevates, empowers, catalyzes).
